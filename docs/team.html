The Story

CodeTracker Pro was born out of frustration. I kept starting LeetCode streaks and losing them. No existing tool felt personal enough. Everything was either too complex or too boring.

So I built the tool I wished existed. Simple. Fast. Fun. A Chrome extension that just works in the background while I focus on solving problems.

What started as a weekend project turned into something bigger. Custom reminders. Streak protection. Auto-detection. Hinglish messages that roast me when I'm being lazy. Every feature exists because I needed it.

Now it's here for you. Free. Private. Made with love.

The Story

CodeTracker Pro was born out of frustration. We kept starting LeetCode streaks and losing them. No existing tool felt personal enough. Everything was either too complex or too boring.

So we built the tool we wished existed. Simple. Fast. Fun. A Chrome extension that just works in the background while we focus on solving problems.

What started as a weekend project turned into something bigger. Custom reminders. Streak protection. Auto-detection. Hinglish messages that roast us when we're being lazy. Every feature exists because we needed it.

Now it's here for you. Free. Private. Made with love.
